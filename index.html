Built to order

Design your own cart

The catalogue is a starting point, not a fixed menu. If you want a cart that is not on this list, we will build it — the thing most clients remember their event for is usually the one we made for them.

  • A regional cart for one cuisine. Awadhi, Gujarati, Bengali, Rajasthani, Goan, Kashmiri or a specific regional street food line.
  • A nostalgia cart for your guests. Pickles, khari, old-school mithai, a regional sweet counter, or your family\'s own recipe.
  • A themed counter to match the function. Bollywood, vintage Delhi, cricket, Holi colours, or a colour palette from your décor.
  • A diet-specific counter. Jain, vegan, gluten-free, low-sugar or kids-allergy-safe, built as its own cart.
  • Something nobody else will have. Tell us the idea. If it can be served from a cart safely and legally, we will work out how.

Custom carts need more lead time than the standard catalogue. Tell us at least two weeks ahead where possible.

Where we work

Service areas

Located in Delhi, serving events anywhere in India. Within Delhi NCR there is no travel charge; beyond it, only travel and crew time are added, quoted in writing first.

Delhi NCR Home base

    No travel charge within NCR, on any day of the week.

    Destination events All India

      From Agra to Jaipur to the hills — we take the carts wherever the event is. Travel and crew time are quoted in writing before you commit.

      Good to know

      • Dates Wedding season books out early. Haldi and mehndi weekdays are usually the most available.
      • Venue access We need power, water and a flat level spot per cart. Worth checking with your venue early.
      • Timing Counters are usually live for two to three hours. Peak queue time is the first hour.
      • Minimum No fixed minimum guest count, but a cart serving under 60 guests costs more per head.
      • Guest dietary needs Tell us about Jain, vegan and allergy requirements when you enquire so the menu can be planned around them.
      • Fire and power Live-flame and heavy-load carts need venue clearance. We handle the paperwork where required.
